SATHVIK GONEPUDI — BASEBALL RECRUITING WEBSITE

Open index.html with the media files beside it. The gallery starts with the
newest capture date and shows media with an unknown date at the bottom.

Add photos and clips through the same ChatGPT conversation. Attach the files
and give each file or batch a game date, such as 2026-10-18, and an optional
caption. A year alone is enough to place a photo or clip in its year group.

To update the gallery by hand, place the new photo or video beside index.html
and copy a matching photo or video card in the gallery markup. Update its
file names, title, alt text or accessible label, and data-date value. Use
YYYY-MM-DD for a known date, YYYY for a known year, or leave data-date empty.
For a video, provide the poster image or remove the poster attribute.
The gallery sorts these cards automatically.

To publish on GitHub, upload index.html and the media files to the root of
main. Edit profile changes stay in the browser until a downloaded update is
uploaded to GitHub.
